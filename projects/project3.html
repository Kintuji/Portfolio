🏆 Global Game Jam 2026 Winner — Etic_Algarve, Faro

Nothin' SUS Here

A 3D multiplayer game where you disguise yourself among a crowd of NPCs, find your opponent hiding in plain sight, and strike without raising suspicion — built in under 48 hours for Global Game Jam 2026.

Unity / C# AI Programmer 1v1 Party Game Global Game Jam 2026

Recognition

Our team won the Global Game Jam at Etic_Algarve, Faro. Off the back of that win, we wanted to show what we'd built to more people, so we brought it to a showcase event hosted by a local gaming hub — people enjoyed it a lot, largely because it's such a simple, funny 1v1 game to pick up and play against a friend on the spot.

Overview

Nothin' SUS Here started as a Global Game Jam 2026 project built around the theme "Mask." From that, we explored identity and disguise through one core mechanic: hiding in plain sight. It's a 3D multiplayer game where both players blend into a crowd of NPCs, and the goal is to find each other in the middle of that crowd and attack strategically — without giving yourself away.

The game has three scenarios, each paired with characters wearing animal masks, which reinforces the disguise theme and keeps every round feeling a little different and a little ridiculous.

My Contributions

I was the AI programmer and responsible for all of the game's AI — and on a game about telling players apart from NPCs, that AI had one job: not look like AI.

  • Player-mimicking NPC behaviour — the core design goal was making the NPCs move and emote like an actual player would, so the real opponent could blend into the crowd and other players couldn't just eyeball who was human.
  • Walking and rotation that reads as human — this was the hardest part of the jam for me: getting NPC movement and turning to look natural and player-like rather than robotic, since anything stiff or mechanical would instantly give away which characters were AI.
  • Random waypoint movement — NPCs patrol between random points around each scenario, so their movement pattern doesn't repeat in a way players could learn and exploit to tell them apart from a real opponent.
  • Built entirely within the jam's 48-hour window — all of the above, designed and implemented under a hard time limit alongside the rest of the team building the scenarios, masks, and UI in parallel.

What Makes It Special

The core mechanic is simple, but that simplicity is exactly what opens up room to expand it later — mini-games, power-ups, and more. Pairing the animal masks with different scenarios creates funny, memorable combinations round to round. And above all, it's fast to learn but genuinely competitive, built for quick sessions where the social back-and-forth between two players is the main event. Simple mechanics, big impact.

Gameplay

Technical Focus

AI designed to be mistaken for a human player — natural-looking movement and rotation, randomised waypoint patrol, and emotes — all built and polished under a 48-hour jam deadline.

Where It's Headed

The team's aim is to keep growing Nothin' SUS Here beyond the jam version — more polish on levels, art, NPCs, and especially the UI, with Steam as the first release target. We think it has real potential as a party/live-service game: something friends pick up for a quick, competitive, family-friendly session.
